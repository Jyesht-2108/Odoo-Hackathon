# General Leave Policy (PTO)

Employees are entitled to 20 days of Paid Time Off (PTO) per calendar year. 
PTO requests must be submitted through the portal at least 7 days in advance. 
PTO cannot be carried over into the next calendar year; any unused balance expires on December 31st. 
Unpaid leave can be granted at the manager's discretion for exceptional circumstances. 
Employees must not take leave if it conflicts with major project deadlines (as tracked in the system) without explicit HR approval.
