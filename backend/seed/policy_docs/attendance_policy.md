# Attendance and Working Hours Policy

Standard working hours are from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday. A total of 8 hours per day is expected. 
Employees must check in via the HR portal upon starting work and check out when finished. 
Any hours worked beyond the 8-hour daily requirement are logged as extra hours, but require prior approval from a manager to be eligible for overtime compensation. 
Weekend check-ins are strongly discouraged. Working on weekends or consistently logging long extra hours will flag a proactive burnout risk alert to the HR team.
