# Sick Leave Policy

Employees receive 10 sick days per calendar year. 
Sick leave does not require prior approval, but employees must notify their manager as soon as possible on the day of absence. 
If sick leave exceeds 3 consecutive days, a medical certificate must be uploaded to the portal as an attachment. 
Unused sick days can be carried over up to a maximum of 5 days into the next calendar year. Frequent sick leaves without medical certificates (e.g. consistently taken on Mondays or Fridays) will trigger a pattern review by HR.
